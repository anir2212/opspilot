# Payment dependency unavailable

## Signal and scope
This runbook applies to the local `payments` demo service. Symptoms: Connection refused or timeout from payment dependency. Record the alert start time, affected requests, current release, and related services before suggesting a cause. Treat this document as guidance, not authorization to change a live system.

## Investigation
Likely cause to test: Dependency endpoint unavailable or connection pool exhausted. Check dependency health and DNS; inspect connection pool errors; keep retry budget bounded; notify dependency owner. Link each observation to its Prometheus query, trace, backup job, or incident number. Compare the signal against a healthy interval; a coincident deploy alone does not prove causation.

## Recovery and verification
Any state-changing step requires a human-approved change request. Synthetic payment request succeeds without retry storm. Record the exact time, owner, and evidence after verification. If the expected recovery does not occur, reopen the hypothesis and escalate.
