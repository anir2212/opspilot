# Payment rollback decision

## Signal and scope
This runbook applies to the local `payments` demo service. Symptoms: Errors begin shortly after a payment service release. Record the alert start time, affected requests, current release, and related services before suggesting a cause. Treat this document as guidance, not authorization to change a live system.

## Investigation
Likely cause to test: New payment version introduced regression. Verify deploy timestamp against error onset; compare canary and stable instances; collect failing trace; draft a rollback request with owner sign-off. Link each observation to its Prometheus query, trace, backup job, or incident number. Compare the signal against a healthy interval; a coincident deploy alone does not prove causation.

## Recovery and verification
Any state-changing step requires a human-approved change request. After approved rollback, errors drop and smoke payment passes. Record the exact time, owner, and evidence after verification. If the expected recovery does not occur, reopen the hypothesis and escalate.
