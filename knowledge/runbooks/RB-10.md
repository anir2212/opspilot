# Retry storm

## Signal and scope
This runbook applies to the local `gateway` demo service. Symptoms: Request rate and retries rise while successful checkouts fall. Record the alert start time, affected requests, current release, and related services before suggesting a cause. Treat this document as guidance, not authorization to change a live system.

## Investigation
Likely cause to test: Retries magnify a failing dependency. Graph original requests versus retries; confirm circuit breaker state; cap retry attempts through approved config change. Link each observation to its Prometheus query, trace, backup job, or incident number. Compare the signal against a healthy interval; a coincident deploy alone does not prove causation.

## Recovery and verification
Any state-changing step requires a human-approved change request. Retry ratio falls while accepted checkouts recover. Record the exact time, owner, and evidence after verification. If the expected recovery does not occur, reopen the hypothesis and escalate.
