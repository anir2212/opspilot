# Gateway latency

## Signal and scope
This runbook applies to the local `gateway` demo service. Symptoms: p95 request latency above two seconds with low throughput. Record the alert start time, affected requests, current release, and related services before suggesting a cause. Treat this document as guidance, not authorization to change a live system.

## Investigation
Likely cause to test: Slow downstream requests or an overloaded gateway worker. Inspect gateway p50 and p95; compare payment latency; sample traces; limit expensive retries; ask owner before changing capacity. Link each observation to its Prometheus query, trace, backup job, or incident number. Compare the signal against a healthy interval; a coincident deploy alone does not prove causation.

## Recovery and verification
Any state-changing step requires a human-approved change request. p95 falls below alert threshold for ten minutes. Record the exact time, owner, and evidence after verification. If the expected recovery does not occur, reopen the hypothesis and escalate.
