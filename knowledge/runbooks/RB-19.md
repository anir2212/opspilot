# Gateway readiness

## Signal and scope
This runbook applies to the local `gateway` demo service. Symptoms: Instances marked unready after startup. Record the alert start time, affected requests, current release, and related services before suggesting a cause. Treat this document as guidance, not authorization to change a live system.

## Investigation
Likely cause to test: Startup dependency or misconfigured readiness path. Inspect readiness probe failures and startup logs; compare environment config; do not send traffic until checks pass. Link each observation to its Prometheus query, trace, backup job, or incident number. Compare the signal against a healthy interval; a coincident deploy alone does not prove causation.

## Recovery and verification
Any state-changing step requires a human-approved change request. All replicas ready and canary request succeeds. Record the exact time, owner, and evidence after verification. If the expected recovery does not occur, reopen the hypothesis and escalate.
