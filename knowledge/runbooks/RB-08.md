# Backup capacity exhausted

## Signal and scope
This runbook applies to the local `backups` demo service. Symptoms: Disk free metric at zero or write failure. Record the alert start time, affected requests, current release, and related services before suggesting a cause. Treat this document as guidance, not authorization to change a live system.

## Investigation
Likely cause to test: Backup repository capacity exhausted. Check free space and retention; identify safe expired snapshots; ask storage owner before deletion; extend capacity through approved change. Link each observation to its Prometheus query, trace, backup job, or incident number. Compare the signal against a healthy interval; a coincident deploy alone does not prove causation.

## Recovery and verification
Any state-changing step requires a human-approved change request. Free space and a test backup write remain healthy. Record the exact time, owner, and evidence after verification. If the expected recovery does not occur, reopen the hypothesis and escalate.
